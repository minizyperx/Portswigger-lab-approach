How to detect SQL:-

 -> Submit single quote character ' and look for errors or other anomalies. 
 -> Boolean conditions such as OR 1=1 and OR 1=2, and look for differences in the application's responses. 

e.g: https://insecure-website.com/products?category=Gifts
changes made: https://insecure-website.com/products?category=Gifts'--

NOTE: -- is a comment in SQL, so anything after it is ignored by the database.

#1 Lab: SQL injection vulnerability in WHERE clause allowing retrieval of hidden data:-
  
    1. modify the category parameter
    2. then add '+OR+1=1-- at the end of the category parameter
    3. then solved

     LOGIC USED:- Always check for '-- or ' at end of parameter and check behavior
